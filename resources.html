Multi-Course Accessibility Audit Protocol

Applied across 34 post-secondary courses in STEM, nursing and health sciences, business, and humanities

A structured method for auditing digital course shells against WCAG 2.1 Level AA and producing a prioritised remediation sequence rather than an undifferentiated list of errors. Classifies failures by type and severity so an institution can decide what to fix first.

Bloom's-AI Integration Model

A human-in-the-loop protocol for coursework in the era of generative AI

Rather than prohibiting AI use or permitting it without structure, assignments are rebuilt so that what gets assessed is the student's engagement with the output: prompting, identifying defects, and explaining corrections. Mapped to Bloom's Taxonomy so the cognitive demand is explicit at each level.

STEM Accessibility Remediation Guides

The document types automated tools handle worst — written for instructors, not developers

  • MathML and LaTeX protocol — moving equations out of images so they are machine-readable and reflow on a phone
  • Structural alternative text for scientific diagrams — describing molecular structure, connectivity, and geometry rather than describing a picture
  • UML, schema, and code-block alternative text — for computing courses
  • Anatomical and laboratory diagram guidance — including how to write alt text that does not disclose assessment answers
