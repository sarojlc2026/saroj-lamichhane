Case Studies and Outcomes

Some of the following I carried out. Some was carried out by faculty I have never met, using published materials. Both matter, and I have marked which is which.

Systemic accessibility audit — 34 courses

Texas A&M University-Texarkana · work I performed

An audit of 34 distinct post-secondary courses spanning STEM (BIOL 466, CHEM 1305, EE 340), health and nursing (NURS 332, NURS 417, NURS 541), business (MGT 395, SCM 310, ACCT 430, MKT 445), and humanities and social sciences (WGSS 1301, HIST 1302, PSCI 2305).

Each course was logged and classified for critical failures: image-only untagged PDFs unreadable by screen readers, static embedded slide decks blocking screen-reader access to text, and instructional video lacking descriptive titles, transcripts, or accurate captioning. The output was a course-by-course remediation roadmap with before-and-after compliance measurement.

Course redesign — UNIV 1100 University Foundation

Texas A&M University-Texarkana · work I performed

Conversion of the University Foundation course to fully online asynchronous delivery across 22 sections, holding to WCAG 2.1 Level AA throughout. A first-year student success course, so quality has direct retention consequences.

Of 171 students surveyed, 81.3% confirmed the redesign and its mentor-led support framework enhanced their academic success.

Independent adoption — University of North Texas

Carried out by Dr. Marcus Vance, Associate Professor of Computer Science and Engineering · I was not involved

Dr. Vance encountered the Bloom's-AI Integration Model and the STEM accessibility guidelines through a conference presentation, retrieved the published materials, and applied them across his own online sections enrolling 140 students. We have never met.

He reports uncritical AI copy-pasting on programming projects falling from roughly 28% to under 4%, Blackboard Ally document accessibility scores rising from 54% to 96%, and final project success rates rising from a historical 78% to 92%. These are his observations in his own courses over one semester.

Independent adoption — University of Pikeville, Kentucky

Carried out by Professor Amitava Bose Bapi · I was not involved

A small private college in Central Appalachia with no instructional design division and no accessibility office. Professor Bapi implemented the Bloom's-AI model in his database courses and used the audit method to bring his syllabus, slides, and coding lab instructions to WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

This is the institutional category the work is ultimately for: colleges where the person who has to comply is the professor teaching the class.

Independent adoption — Millville Area School District, Pennsylvania

Carried out by Dr. Humendra Paudel · I was not involved

A Ph.D. chemist teaching high school science applied the MathML remediation protocol, structural alternative text across more than 120 molecular diagrams, and the Bloom's-AI lesson planning templates across sections enrolling 85 students. Evidence that the methods transfer outside higher education entirely.
