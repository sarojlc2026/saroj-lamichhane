About

I am Curriculum Coordinator in Institutional Effectiveness, Research, and Planning at Texas A&M University-Texarkana, where I previously served as Instructional Designer II.

My work sits at an unusual intersection. Most instructional designers are trained in qualitative educational theory; most data analysts work in business settings. I hold a graduate degree in analytics and doctoral training in higher education, which means I treat accessibility compliance as a measurable institutional problem — something you can audit, quantify, and prove progress on — rather than something addressed course by course by impression.
