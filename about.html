About

Saroj Lamichhane is Curriculum Coordinator in Institutional Effectiveness, Research, and Planning at Texas A&M University-Texarkana, having previously served there as Instructional Designer II.

The work sits at an unusual intersection. Most instructional designers are trained in qualitative educational theory; most data analysts work in business settings. A graduate degree in analytics combined with doctoral training in higher education produces a different approach: accessibility compliance treated as a measurable institutional problem, something that can be audited, quantified and shown to improve, rather than something addressed course by course by impression.
